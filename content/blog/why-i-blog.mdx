There's a particular feeling I get when I try to explain an idea out loud and it falls apart halfway through. The sentence starts confidently, and then the middle just... isn't there. I've learned to trust that feeling. It means the idea isn't finished.

This blog is my workshop for finishing ideas.

## Thinking on paper

I don't write to share what I know. I write to discover what I know. Most of what you'll find here started as a scrappy note — a question, an itch, a bug that wouldn't leave me alone — and only became an essay because I sat down and chased it to its end.

The act of writing forces a kind of honesty that thinking alone rarely achieves. An idea in your head can hide behind a vague phrase. On paper, vague doesn't survive contact with a reader.

## What this space is for

Three kinds of posts, roughly:

1. **Write-ups of projects** — the thinking behind the build, not just the demo.
2. **Notes on tools and techniques** — things I learned the slow way so you don't have to.
3. **Occasional essays** — the ideas that wouldn't fit anywhere else.

If any of it helps you, or makes you argue with me, that's a good day. I'd rather be disagreed with than skimmed past.
